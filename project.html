Research Topic

Technology in health tracking devices explores how wearable technology helps people monitor their health, physical activity, sleep, and recovery. This project focuses on three popular wearable devices: Oura Ring, Apple Watch, and WHOOP. It examines their features, technology, and usefulness in everyday health monitoring.

Research Subtopics

  • Oura Ring: Smart Ring Technology and Health Monitoring
  • Apple Watch: Smartwatch Technology and Personal Health Tracking
  • WHOOP: Wearable Fitness Technology, Sleep, and Recovery Tracking

Additional Project Documents

Open Research Paper Open Presentation

Project Purpose

The purpose of this research is to understand how wearable health devices collect information and help people become more aware of their health. It also explores the benefits and limitations of health tracking technology and the importance of accurate health measurements.
